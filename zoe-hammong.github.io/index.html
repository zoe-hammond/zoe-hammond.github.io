About Me!

My name is Zoe Hammond and I am a senior at the University of Maryland in College Park. My major is journalism with a focus in sports writing with hopes to find a career that will not only allow me to apply my knowledge and experiences, but also expand them. My courses here at UMD have allowed me to learn more about journalism besides writing. I have experience with videography such as recording one to three minute news shorts and broadcast writing. While at Maryland, I have written for a number of student sports publications. With Terrapin Sports Central, I covered Maryland mens soccer throughout its 2025 season. During 2026, I have covered Maryland baseball for the Maryland Baseball Network and currently I am a baseball beat writer for Testudo Times.
